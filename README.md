# Silhouette

A top-down pixel-art survival game that runs in the browser. You are a shadow with twin daggers in a ruined castle. Survive 15 waves of the old king's soldiers, then defeat your own betraying clone to claim the throne.

The run starts outside the walls on a rainy, moonlit night. Wave 1 is the gatehouse, wave 2 the castle yard and wave 3 the graveyard. After that you fight up through the castle, one room per wave, and no two runs go quite the same way:
- Ten of the fifteen castle rooms are picked for each run, in a loose order: cellars and barracks tend to come early, and the ballroom and battlements late. The rooms are the dungeon cells, the wine cellar, the barracks, the kitchen, the armory, the banquet hall, the library, the scriptorium, the long gallery, the alchemy lab, the chapel, the crypt, the treasury, the ballroom and the rain-soaked battlements.
- Wave 8 is always the guard hall, and wave 15 is always the royal antechamber.
- Most rooms have two or three furniture layouts, and one is picked at random each time you enter.
- The order is kept for the whole run, through deaths and reloads.

Outside, rain ripples the puddles and splashes under every footstep, and the outdoor areas have hazards of their own:
- The gatehouse sits behind a moat with a single drawbridge. Walking up to the edge just stops you. A hit that knocks you over it, or a roll that ends above the water, drops you in: you climb back out onto the bank, 12 health lighter. Soldiers on the far side have to come round by the bridge, and any soldier you knock into the moat sinks for good.
- Hay carts come barrelling through the side passages of the castle yard. A rumble and a flickering gold lane warn you first. Step out of the lane or roll through the cart. Soldiers caught in the lane get flattened too.
- Patches of mud in the yard, the graveyard and the gatehouse slow anyone wading through, you and the soldiers alike.

Each room has its own layout and furniture to fight around. Archers can't shoot through shelves, statues or pillars, but their arrows fly over tables, pews and tombs. The exit door is sealed until every enemy in the room is dead. Then it opens and you walk through to the next room. The 15th door leads to the throne room.

After each of the first 14 rooms you choose one of three blessing cards. Each card is Common, Rare or Legendary, and rarer cards give bigger numbers. Blessings stack and last the whole run, even through deaths:
- Vitality: more max health
- Keen Edge: more slash damage
- Wellspring: extra energy at the start of each room
- Siphon: stronger heal orbs
- Swiftness: faster movement
- Tumbler: shorter roll cooldown
- Riposte: more energy per parry
- Steady Hand: a longer parry window
- Mending Guard: heal on every parry
- Cruelty: a chance to deal double damage
- Bloodthirst: heal on every kill
- Hunger: the ultimate charges faster
- Focus: cheaper Shadow Blast
- Kinship: the Shadow Clone lasts longer

The pause menu lists your blessings.

The castle fights back in different ways:
- Knights block from the front. Parry one and its shield flies off for good.
- Sappers keep their distance and throw snares onto the floor. A snare is hard to spot (watch for the glint). Step on one and it holds your feet for a moment. Roll across it, or cut it with a slash, a Rending slash or a Blast.
- Mages drop burning circles, or charge a beam that tracks you and then locks on. Step aside once it locks, or roll through it. Tall furniture blocks beams.

Only three melee fighters press in on you at once; the rest circle at a distance and wait for an opening.

From wave 5 on, some soldiers are elites: one per room at first, then two, then three. An elite has a coloured outline and glow, a small diamond over its head, and its trait called out when it arrives. Elites are tougher, and each one drops a heal orb and 20 energy when it falls:
- Burning (orange) leaves a trail of fire. Its fire and its hits set you alight for a few seconds. Roll across the flames to stay clear of them.
- Swift (cyan) moves faster and swings sooner.
- Splitting (green) bursts into two small copies when it dies.
- Vampiric (red) heals whenever it hurts you or your clone.

About one room in three has a twist, announced right after the wave banner. Twists never come twice in a row, and never in the first three waves or the last one:
- Lights out (indoor rooms only): the torches and candles gutter and die as the fight starts. You see by your own glow and the soldiers by their ember eyes. The lights come back once the room is cleared.
- A captain leads them: a big captain with a gold outline and a pennant on its back leads the room. Kill it and every soldier left breaks and runs for the gates. The rest of the wave never comes, and the captain drops two heal orbs and 50 energy.

## The Twin Captains

Wave 8 is a mini-boss fight in the guard hall against two captains who fight as a pair:
- The Shield Captain is a huge knight that blocks from the front. Parry its bash to knock the shield away for a few seconds.
- The Blade Captain darts in for quick flurries of cuts, then falls back behind the shield. It also lunges from a distance: watch for the crouch, then sidestep or parry. After a lunge it is open for a long moment.
- Kill one and the other rages: faster, harder and relentless.

In Rush mode the Twin Captains guard every room ending in 5 (rooms 5, 15, 25 and so on).

No two soldiers are quite alike. Each suit of hollow armor has its own helmet, scrap of cloth, rust, dents, eye glow and build (tall, stocky or hunched). While they wait their turn they fidget: some breathe, some glance around, some tap a foot or flip a dagger. They talk, too: speech bubbles pop up when they spot you, get hit, fall, get parried or cast, and they throw taunts at you mid-fight.

Your twin daggers are black shadow blades wrapped in curling purple tendrils, with threads of shadow drifting off them. Every slash is a stroke of pure shadow ink with a purple rim, and it unravels into wisps as it fades.

Each ability has its own sound:
- Shadow Blast is an implosion: a quick rush of air, then a deep boom.
- The Rending slash is an ink whip.
- The Shadow Clone is a whoosh that splits in two.
- The Shadow veil is a soft hush that fades away.
- The ultimate is a heavy drop under a dark, rising chord.

## Appearance

Choose how your shadow looks from Appearance on the title screen: the classic shade, the hooded wanderer, the broken crown, the masked assassin, the smoke wraith, the horned shade or man face. Man face wears a big round head with a famously smug face, drawn sharp on top of the pixel art so every line of it shows. A preview shows each one walking and slashing. Your choice is saved, and your clone and the Betrayer wear it too.

## Rush mode

Rush mode is an endless run from the title menu. The rooms come in random order and the enemies keep getting tougher. Elites show up from room 3, with one more every 6 rooms (up to four), and room twists can strike from room 4. You have one life and every move from the start, and you choose a blessing after every room. The Twin Captains hold every room ending in 5. Every 10th room is a boss, and the two bosses take turns, coming back stronger each time around. Beating one gives you a choice of three Legendary blessings. Your best room is saved.

- **The Iron Warden** (rooms 10, 30, 50...) is a giant hollow knight that never just swings at you; it uses an ability every few seconds. It lowers its greatsword and charges: parry the impact and its guard breaks, leaving it open to extra damage (or roll aside, and if it hits a wall it reels). It drives the sword into the floor to raise lines of iron pillars toward you; each spot cracks and glows first, so step off the marks. And it leaps and slams the floor: roll just as the shockwave ring reaches you.
- **The Ember Archmage** (rooms 20, 40, 60...) teleports around the room and sweeps a turning beam across it; run ahead of the beam, hide behind a pillar or roll through it. It also rains meteors in waves. It blinks away when you crowd it, but after each spell it is spent for a moment.

Both have three phases and get faster in each. Each has its own theme: hammer-blow drums and anvil clinks for the Warden, rippling ember arpeggios for the Archmage. Both speed up in the last phase.

The throne room boss grows stronger with every blessing you carry in, so a stacked run still gets a real fight. It doesn't slash at you; it uses an ability every couple of seconds. It throws fans of shadow daggers (parry one to send it back into the Betrayer, or roll through). After a red flash at your feet it blinks in behind you and grabs: roll away before the red ring closes or it pins you in place. In its second phase it sends shadow waves rolling across the floor. In its third it splits into shadow copies that lock on and dash through you. Roll through them or step out of the line. Its shadow blast can be rolled too: roll as the red ring closes in.

## Controls

| Input | Action |
| --- | --- |
| W A S D | Move |
| Left click | Slash. Every swing needs its own click; holding the button does nothing more. Click fast to chain the combo (every 3rd swing is a heavy finisher). A click up to 3 frames early, at the end of a roll, a parry or the previous swing, still counts |
| Right click | Parry when the ! above an enemy turns red (+30 energy, up to +50 for parries in a row without getting hit). A good parry is ready again at once, so you can answer every hit of a combo |
| Space / X | Roll: you're untouchable mid-tumble, so arrows and strikes miss. Rolling through an attack that would have hit you gives +25 energy (once per roll) |
| E | Shadow Blast (50 energy) |
| C | Rending slash (25 energy, unlocks on wave 3) |
| R | Shadow Clone (100 energy, unlocks on wave 5) |
| V | Shadow veil (50 energy, unlocks on wave 8) |
| 1 | Ultimate, once the bar is full (20 kills) |
| Esc / P · M | Pause · Sound and music |

These are the default keys. Every action can be moved, so the whole game can be played on the keyboard alone (see Key bindings below).

## Key bindings

Open Key bindings from the title screen or the pause menu (you can change keys mid-run). Every action has two slots, a main and an alternate: move up, down, left and right, slash, parry, roll, Shadow Blast, Rending slash, Shadow Clone, Shadow veil, the ultimate and pause.
- Pick a slot, then press any key or mouse button (left, middle, right or the side buttons). Esc cancels, and Delete clears the slot.
- A key that is already in use swaps places: the action it came from gets the slot's old key, so nothing is ever bound twice.
- Slash and parry work on keys exactly as they do on the mouse. Each key press is one swing, and holding the key does not repeat.
- Esc always pauses and backs out of menus, and M always turns the sound on and off, so those two can't be rebound.
- Reset to defaults brings back the keys in the table above.
- Your bindings are saved in the browser. The HUD key boxes, the controls lists and the in-game tips all show your own keys.

The ! parry warning always draws on top of everything else. If it would land under a HUD panel or another warning, it slides clear and a thin line points back to the enemy it belongs to.

Slash, parry and roll sit in the top right. The moves that cost energy sit in a bar at the bottom center, with your energy underneath. Shadow Blast is there from the start; each other move unlocks with a banner when you reach its wave:
- Rending slash: lunge forward and cut through every enemy in a line. It staggers them and knights can't block it.
- Shadow Clone: summon a shadow that fights beside you for a while.
- Shadow veil: turn nearly invisible for 3 seconds. Enemies lose track of you, and your next hit is a critical. The Betrayer only loses you for a moment.

Every 3 kills a green heal orb drops near you. Walk over it for +15 HP and +25 energy before it fades (8 seconds). Energy past 100 isn't wasted: every 25 extra counts as a kill toward your ultimate. The final boss drops one at 75%, 50% and 25% health.

The ultimate bar fills as you kill. Press 1 to wreathe yourself in shadow flame for 30 seconds or until the wave ends. Your slashes reach much farther, you move faster, and a 100-point shield absorbs damage before your health does.

Plays with a mouse and keyboard, or with the keyboard alone after rebinding slash and parry. The whole game is the single file `index.html`.
