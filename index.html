How to play

Controls

WAIT FOR RED · THEN RIGHT CLICK

A yellow ! means an attack is coming. Right click the moment it turns red to parry, counter and earn +30 energy, more for parries in a row; a parry is ready again at once, so you can answer a whole combo. Too early and you still take 70%. Parrying a knight knocks its shield off. Every 3 kills drops a heal orb. 3 lives for the whole run; Rush mode is endless with 1 life.
